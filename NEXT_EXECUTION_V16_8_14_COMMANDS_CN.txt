Benchmark 使用原则（请勿混用）
----------------------------------------
PRIMARY TRAIN:
  scenario/training -> COWP labels / critical / priority / natural / witness
  tf_example/training -> matched model tensors + Waymax

PRIMARY VALIDATION:
  scenario/validation + matched tf_example/validation
  -> smoke/strict/held-out validation

SECONDARY INTERACTION STRESS:
  validation_interactive
  -> 先审 scenario-ID overlap，再单独报告；不替换 standard validation，不与重叠样本相加。

DO NOT BUILD FUTURE-DEPENDENT COWP LABELS FROM:
  testing
  testing_interactive
  （official test future GT hidden）

DO NOT MIX INTO CURRENT 91-STEP PIPELINE WITHOUT REDESIGN:
  training_20s

Scenario proto 保持 traffic-control/right-of-way label 权威源；
tf.Example 只做 matched tensor/Waymax source，不用其固定128-object/16-light tensor反向重算 priority GT。
